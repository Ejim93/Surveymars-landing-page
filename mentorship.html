🧭 How Mentorship Will Work

When the mentorship system becomes active, members will be able to:

  • Create a mentorship profile.
  • Select areas where they need guidance.
  • Browse suitable mentorship opportunities.
  • Submit a mentorship request or application.
  • Track the status of their application.
  • Receive important notifications.

🔐 Safety & Verification

SurveyMars will prioritize verified mentorship opportunities and appropriate protection for members.

Mentorship providers will eventually go through an approval process before their opportunities become publicly available.
