🧭 How Mentorship Will Work

When the mentorship system becomes active, SurveyMars will guide members through a simple process:

1️⃣ Create Your Profile

Tell us about your interests, goals and the areas where you would like to receive guidance.

2️⃣ Discover Suitable Mentors

Browse available mentors and mentorship opportunities that match your interests and goals.

3️⃣ Send a Mentorship Request

Submit a request or application to a suitable mentorship opportunity.

4️⃣ Learn, Connect & Grow

Once approved, members can participate in mentorship activities and track their progress.

🔔 Stay Updated

Important application updates, approvals and mentorship notifications can eventually appear through your SurveyMars account.

🔐 Safety, Trust & Verification

SurveyMars is being designed with member safety and trustworthy mentorship at the center of the experience.

✅ Verified Mentors

Mentors will go through an approval process before their mentorship opportunities can be made available to members.

🛡️ Member Protection

SurveyMars will establish guidelines designed to help protect members from misleading, inappropriate or fraudulent mentorship activities.

🚩 Reporting & Review

Members will eventually have a way to report concerns about mentorship opportunities or interactions that do not follow SurveyMars guidelines.

🔒 Privacy

Personal information will be handled according to SurveyMars privacy policies and the security controls supporting the platform.

👨‍💼 Admin Review

Mentorship opportunities will be subject to administrative review before being made available to members.
